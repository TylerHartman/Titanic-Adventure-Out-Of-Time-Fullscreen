Titanic: Adventure Out of Time — Final Fullscreen Builds

Choose ONE executable:

  Titanic_Fullscreen.exe
    Edge-to-edge fullscreen. The original 4:3 image is stretched to fill the display.

  Titanic_4x3.exe
    Aspect-correct 4:3 fullscreen. Uses full screen height with centered black
    pillar bars on widescreen displays. Mouse/hotspot mapping is adjusted to match.

  Titanic_Cinematic_Fullscreen.exe
    Edge-to-edge fullscreen with cinematic expansion. Normal gameplay uses the
    same fullscreen presentation as Titanic_Fullscreen.exe. When a 512x264 movie
    is playing and the lower menu/HUD area is absent, the movie itself is
    stretched to fill the entire display.

All builds retain the final feature set, including:
- modern fullscreen compositor and mouse mapping
- Windows system-color corruption fix
- process affinity pinned to logical processor 0

The 4:3 executable is made directly from the final fullscreen executable.
Only presentation geometry and inverse mouse mapping differ.

The Cinematic Fullscreen executable uses the normal fullscreen presentation
during gameplay and expands qualifying cinematics to the full display.
